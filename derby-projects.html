Attendance Tracker & Skater Management System

A custom, web-based system designed for Greenville Roller Derby to track attendance, eligibility, and volunteer participation across teams, seasons, and game windows.

Role: Product Designer · Front-End Developer · Systems Architect

Tools: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script

Context: 501(c)(3) nonprofit sports organization

Project Overview

I designed and built a centralized attendance and eligibility platform to replace manual spreadsheets and fragmented workflows. The system supports skaters, coaches, and administrators through a mobile-friendly portal that prioritizes clarity, accuracy, and ease of use.

Core Features

  • Clock-in / clock-out attendance tracking with validation
  • Missed punch submissions and automatic punch pairing
  • Attendance windows tied to game schedules
  • Practice blackout dates and Sunday limits enforced automatically
  • Dynamic eligibility calculations per skater and team
  • Centralized skater database with multi-team filtering

Volunteer Hours Module

Volunteer hours are tracked as a separate but integrated module within the same hub. Administrators can view monthly and window-based summaries, identify shortfalls, and clearly communicate requirements to skaters.

  • Imported and normalized volunteer data
  • Automatic completion status and shortfall detection
  • Clear, auditable reporting views
  • Reduced disputes through transparent requirements

Design & UX

  • Designed as a cohesive internal web app, not just spreadsheets
  • Reusable UI components and shared design tokens
  • Responsive layouts optimized for long names and dense data
  • Iterative refinement based on real league usage
  • Embedded into an existing league hub with no installs or logins

Technical Highlights

  • Google Apps Script backend for validation and calculations
  • Advanced Google Sheets formulas (ARRAYFORMULA, QUERY, FILTER, LET, IFS)
  • Client-side filtering and dynamic rendering
  • Modular architecture designed for future expansion

Impact

  • Reduced administrative overhead and manual calculations
  • Improved accuracy, trust, and transparency
  • Made complex league policies understandable and enforceable
  • Created a foundation for a future league CMS
